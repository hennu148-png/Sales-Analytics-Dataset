# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization and business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, regional sales, and profitability.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights through interactive charts, KPIs, filters, and visualizations. It enables users to monitor key business metrics, identify sales trends, understand customer contributions, and evaluate product and regional performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Track revenue, orders, quantity sold, and customers.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Analyze profit distribution by region.
- Identify top customers based on revenue.
- Compare sales and costs across products.
- Analyze sales by item category.
- Evaluate quantity sold by product.
- Monitor order volume over time.
- Provide actionable insights through an interactive dashboard.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 💰 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides an overview of important business metrics:

- **Total Revenue**
- **Total Quantity Sold**
- **Total Orders**
- **Average Order Value**
- **Total Customers**

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of the overall sales performance.

### 📅 Monthly Sales Trend

The monthly sales trend visualizes revenue performance across different months, helping identify:

- Sales growth and decline.
- Seasonal patterns.
- High-performing months.
- Low-performing months.

### 🌍 Sales by Region

The regional sales analysis compares sales performance across:

- East
- North
- South
- West

This helps identify the regions generating the highest sales.

### 💹 Profit by Region

A donut chart provides a visual comparison of profit contribution across different regions, allowing users to understand regional profitability.

### 🏆 Top 5 Customers by Revenue

The dashboard identifies the top five customers based on revenue contribution, helping businesses understand their most valuable customers.

### 📦 Sales vs Cost by Product

This analysis compares sales revenue and associated costs across different products, helping evaluate product-level profitability and performance.

### 🥧 Sales by Item Category

The dashboard analyzes sales distribution across different product categories, including:

- Backpack
- Chair
- Charger
- Desk Lamp
- Notebook
- Pen Set
- USB Drive

### 📊 Quantity Sold by Product

This visualization compares the quantity sold for each product and helps identify products with the highest sales volume.

### 📈 Orders Over Time

The orders-over-time chart tracks order volume across different months, making it easier to identify changes in customer purchasing activity.
